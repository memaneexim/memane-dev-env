Our Story

About Memane
International

Memane International was founded with a single belief: that India's quality products deserve to reach every corner of the world. Starting from Pune, Maharashtra — our founder Tejas Memane built a company rooted in trust, transparency, and delivering genuine value in global trade.

We are a growing import–export startup with strong links to suppliers across India — engaged in the global trade of food products, commodities, minerals, and selected engineering goods.

Our product range spans: fresh fruits & vegetables, grains & cereals, spices, dairy, frozen foods, edible oils, minerals, engineering products, and cashew imports from Africa.

"To deliver the finest products from India to the world — with integrity, consistency, and care."
— Tejas Memane, Founder & Proprietor
